# Publishing films and the skill

The skill's own code is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). That licence covers this
package, not what you make with it. Before publishing a film or a changed
package, establish the rights to its source material, fonts, model output
(check each model's licence) and any reference voice (the speaker's permission),
and choose a licence for your own additions. Removing identity markers does not
establish those rights.

Review filenames, docs, metadata, URLs, environment variables, source, images,
video pixels, spoken audio and generated receipts. Keep source archives, private
assessments, credentials, browser state, reference recordings and product assets
outside a public package. Do not ship work-specific examples under neutral names.

The example card is newly authored neutral HTML. No recorded voice, model
weights or product capture is bundled. Optional provider and browser paths
belong in production configuration, not in a redistributed skill.
