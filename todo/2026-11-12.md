# 12 November 2026

## What We Built

Narrate now introduces itself with a short setup sequence followed by real English and Arabic voice samples. The website, captions, poster and downloadable skill present the same updated experience.

Agents can make clearer videos with sharper captures, camera emphasis, readable captions, optional generated music and sound effects, and consistent screen colours. Saved voices can be checked and reused without repeating setup.

## What We Fixed

English recording uses the complete original passage and encourages a natural pace. The misleading five-to-ten-second target and suggestions to shorten accepted recordings are removed. Earlier recordings remain preserved, and pronunciation aids remain available for other languages.

Narration guidance preserves preferred reference recordings and connected phrasing. Media checks catch ordered speech differences, missing glyphs, caption issues and export failures. The standalone site uses its own footer links and a neutral language icon, keeping unrelated branding out of the delivered experience.

## Key Decisions

The intro stays focused on setup, English and Arabic. Both samples use the creator-authorized original English voice reference. New sample listening approval remains pending and is labelled honestly.

Private recordings, model weights, raw captures and production receipts remain outside Git. Local verification continues to replace hosted automation; automatic deployment remains disabled. This release commits and pushes source and finished website media without deploying the website.

## Breaking Changes

The studio-film production helper now requires explicit plan and output paths. Existing saved voice profiles remain supported.
