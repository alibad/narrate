# Narrate identity

The owner selected Narrate and narrate.humanquest.net, and asked for a choice
between exploration directions 03 and 04. Direction 03 was selected: the story
ribbon passing through a video frame. It connects narration with a real screen
without relying on a microphone, globe, flag or generic play-button logo.

The five-direction contact sheet was produced with the built-in image-generation
tool and copied into this project as logo-explorations.png. The selected direction
was drawn as editable SVG for interface, favicon, app icon and social usage.

Website palette: paper #f7f5ef, ink #161b24, coral #ed503d.
Local studio: deep ink surfaces, a warm coral accent and measured typography.
Waveform displays derive from actual saved audio and follow its playback cursor;
only active microphone recording animates the recording indicator. The five-step
workflow is unchanged. Reduced-motion settings suppress decorative transitions.

Exploration prompt: five polished, distinct Narrate directions—folded N/frame,
speaking aperture, story thread through frame, subtitle-line N, and editorial
speech/play mark—in ink, cream, coral and optional lilac. Avoid microphone,
globe, flag and equalizer clichés, generic gradients and 3D mockups.
